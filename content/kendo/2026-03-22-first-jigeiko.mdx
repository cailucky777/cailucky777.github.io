First time sparring in full bogu. I expected to be scared of being hit; the actual problem was that I forgot to *breathe*. By the third match my men was foggy and my arms were lead.

Things I want to remember:

- Distance is everything. From issoku-itto-no-maai, one solid step is enough.
- A bad men cut beats a hesitant nothing.
- Reset between matches. Wipe the men, drink water, show up to the next opponent with a clean head.

Sensei said: *"You attacked. That's the entire point of tonight."*
