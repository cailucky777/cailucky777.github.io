Whole class focused on spine articulation through the bridge series. The cue that finally landed: "stack vertebrae like coins, don't lift like a plank."

What worked:

- Light spring on footwork — better feedback for asymmetry.
- Long box pulling straps, eyes soft on the ceiling, ribs *down*.
- Final stretch on the short box: hip flexors finally let go.

What to keep working on:

- Right shoulder still rolls forward at the top of the bridge.
- Breath cadence on the hundred — lazy on the exhale.
