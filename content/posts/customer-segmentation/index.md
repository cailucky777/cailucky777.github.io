**Authors**: **Savina (Luying) Cai**

Performs customer segmentation analysis on mall shopping data using unsupervised learning techniques. Applies K-Means, DBSCAN, and hierarchical clustering with optimal cluster selection via silhouette analysis and the elbow method. Generates actionable customer personas for targeted marketing strategies, demonstrating practical business analytics applications of ML.
