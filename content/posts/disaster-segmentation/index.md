**Authors**: **Savina (Luying) Cai**

Applies U-Net architecture for pixel-level semantic segmentation on the RescueNet dataset to assess natural disaster damage from aerial imagery. The model classifies terrain into multiple damage categories, enabling rapid post-disaster assessment for emergency response teams. Evaluates encoder architectures, loss functions, and augmentation strategies to optimize segmentation quality on imbalanced damage classes.
