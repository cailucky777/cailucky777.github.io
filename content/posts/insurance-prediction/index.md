**Authors**: **Savina (Luying) Cai**, et al.

Comparative study on predicting auto insurance claims using LightGBM and ensemble methods on highly imbalanced datasets. Investigates sampling strategies (SMOTE, ADASYN, undersampling), cost-sensitive learning, and threshold calibration to optimize the precision-recall tradeoff for fraud detection. Demonstrates practical ML deployment considerations for financial services.
