**Authors**: **Savina (Luying) Cai**

Develops a deep learning pipeline for automated pneumonia detection from chest X-ray images. Implements custom CNN architectures and transfer learning strategies in PyTorch, with systematic evaluation through ablation studies, data augmentation experiments, and learning rate scheduling. Achieves clinically relevant classification accuracy on the Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia) dataset, demonstrating practical applications of deep learning in medical diagnostics.
