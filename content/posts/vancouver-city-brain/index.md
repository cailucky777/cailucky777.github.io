**Authors**: **Savina (Luying) Cai**, et al.

Developed an urban intelligence system combining geospatial machine learning with interactive data visualization to analyze city-scale datasets for Vancouver. Engineered data pipelines for spatial analysis, built predictive models for urban pattern recognition, and created decision-support dashboards for resource optimization and urban planning. Demonstrates ability to work with large-scale, real-world geospatial data and translate ML insights into actionable urban policy recommendations.
